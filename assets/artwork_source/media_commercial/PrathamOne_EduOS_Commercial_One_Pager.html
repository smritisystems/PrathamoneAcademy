❌ Operational Failure Points in Coaching
  • Revenue Leakage: Manual discount promises, forgotten installments, untracked post-dated cheques (PDCs).
  • Accidental Data Loss: Front-desk staff modifying or deleting records in Excel or unlocked software.
  • Faculty Disputes: Monthly arguments over hourly/session lectures and compensation deductions.
  • Parent App Fatigue: Parents refuse to install or log in to separate coaching mobile apps.
