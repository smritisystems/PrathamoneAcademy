# Admissions Module User Manual

**Target Audience**: Admission Counselors, Branch Administrators, Office Staff  

---

## 1. How to Register a New Student

### Step 1: Navigate to Registration Wizard
1. Log in to Prashant Academy with your staff or counselor credentials.
2. In the left navigation menu, click **Students**.
3. In the top-right corner of the students table, click **+ Add Student**.

### Step 2: Personal Information (Step 1 of 4)
- **Full Name**: Enter student's full name as per Aadhaar or school records. *(Mandatory)*
- **Date of Birth**: Select using the date picker. *(Mandatory)*
- **Gender**: Select Male, Female, or Other. *(Mandatory)*
- **Blood Group**, **Mother Tongue**, **Medium**: Optional demographic details.
- **Address & Pincode**: Enter residential details for transport planning.
- Click **Next →**.

### Step 3: Guardian Details (Step 2 of 4)
- **Guardian Full Name**: Enter primary parent or legal guardian's name. *(Mandatory)*
- **Relationship**: Choose Father, Mother, Guardian, etc. *(Mandatory)*
- **Mobile Number**: Enter 10-digit mobile number for SMS/WhatsApp notices. *(Mandatory)*
- **Annual Income**: Used for scholarship qualification.
- Click **Next →**.

### Step 4: Academic Placement (Step 3 of 4)
- **Standard**: Select from Standard 1 through Standard 12. *(Mandatory)*
- **Division**: Select designated section (e.g., Division A).
- **Admission Date**: Defaults to today's date.
- Click **Next →**.

### Step 5: Review & Submit (Step 4 of 4)
- Review summary of all entered details.
- Click **✓ Register Student**.
- The system generates an Admission Number (e.g., `PA-2024-000010`) and redirects to the Student Detail profile.
