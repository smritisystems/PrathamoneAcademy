# Reports & Dashboard User Manual

**Target Audience**: Administrators, Accountants, Center Heads, Academic Coordinators  

---

## 1. Using the Coaching Institute Dashboard
1. Navigate to **Dashboard** from the primary sidebar navigation.
2. The system automatically defaults the view to the **Current Academic Year**.
3. **Filter Bar**:
   - Filter by **Academic Year** to review historical or current financial performance.
   - Filter by **Standard / Level** to inspect standard-specific enrollments and fee balances.
   - Filter by **Batch** to narrow down metrics to a specific classroom/division batch.
   - Click **Reset Filters** to restore institute-wide overview.
4. **Key Metrics**:
   - **Total Students**: Active enrolled students matching filter criteria.
   - **Total Fees Invoiced**: Sum of all non-cancelled fee invoice receivables.
   - **Collected Fees**: Total verified payments collected, alongside the real-time collection rate percentage.
   - **Outstanding Balance**: Remaining fee balance due from students.
5. **Monthly Fee Collection Trends**:
   - Visualizes month-over-month revenue collections over the academic session.
   - Hover over any bar to inspect exact collection amounts in ₹.
6. **Recent Payment Transactions**:
   - Displays the last 10 payment transactions with official receipt numbers (`RCP-X-XXXXXX`), student details, batch, method (Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer), amount, and date.
7. **Quick Actions**:
   - Direct shortcuts to Enroll Student (`+ Enroll Student`), Fee Management, Attendance Register, Collection Reports, Batch Allocations, and Standards.

---

## 2. Viewing Fee Collection Reports
1. In the sidebar, click **Reports**.
2. Under the **Fee Collection Report** tab:
   - Review each student's Admission #, Name, Total Fees, Paid, and Outstanding.
   - The **Collection %** pill indicates payment progress (Green: 100%, Blue: >=50%, Yellow: <50%).

---

## 2. Tracking Outstanding Dues
1. Switch to the **Outstanding Dues Report** tab.
2. View pending invoices with invoice number, student name, net billed, paid, balance, and due date.
3. Unpaid and partial invoices are flagged with color-coded status badges.

---

## 3. Exporting to CSV and Excel
1. In the top-right corner of the Reports screen:
   - Click **📥 Export CSV** to download a comma-separated values file for spreadsheets or Tally.
   - Click **📊 Export Excel** to download an Excel workbook.
2. Files are named automatically with timestamp (e.g., `fee_collection_report_1700000000.csv`).
