1. Fast-Track System Setup in 5 Steps

Welcome to PrathamOne EduOS. This Quick Start Guide gets your coaching academy operational in less than 30 minutes. Follow these five core steps:

  1. Step 1: Sign in and Customize Your Institute Branding

    Navigate to http://localhost:80/login. Sign in using your administrator credentials. Immediately go to Settings (/settings) to upload your coaching institute's logo, set your primary brand colors, and verify your institute contact phone and address.

  2. Step 2: Create Educational Boards & Standards

    Open Academic Structure (/academic). Under the Boards tab, add your curriculums (e.g. CBSE, State Board). Switch to the Standards tab and add your class grades (e.g. 9th, 10th, 11th Science, 12th Commerce).

  3. Step 3: Define Batches & Classroom Capacity

    In the Batches tab, create morning or evening batches for each standard. Set classroom seat capacities so the system can monitor intake limits.

  4. Step 4: Configure Fee Packages & Payment Installments

    Open Fees (/fees). Click Create Fee Plan to configure your standard tuition fee packages and installment plans (e.g. Quarterly or Monthly).

  5. Step 5: Admit Students & Issue Identity Cards

    Open Students → Add Student (/students/add). Enter student profile details, select their standard and batch, and submit. The system assigns a permanent registration code and offers instant ID card generation via Forms (/forms).

2. Core Daily Operations

2.1 Daily Attendance Marking

Open /attendance, choose your batch, and verify the roster. All students default to Present. Click any student to toggle to Absent or Late, then click Save Attendance Roster.

Daily Attendance Marking Screen.

2.2 Collecting Fees & Printing Receipts

Go to /payments → click Record Payment. Search for the student. Their pending installments appear automatically. Enter the amount paid, select Cash/UPI/Cheque, enter transaction reference, and click Save & Post. Click Print Receipt to issue an official thermal or A4 receipt.
