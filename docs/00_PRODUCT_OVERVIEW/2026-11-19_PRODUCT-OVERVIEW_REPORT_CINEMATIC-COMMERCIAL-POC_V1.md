## 4. TECHNICAL LIMITATIONS & PRODUCTION BOUNDARIES

Before expanding to the complete 2–4 minute commercial, the following technical constraints must be recognized:

1. **Local vs. Cloud Video Generation:**
   - Advanced diffusion-based photorealistic human video models (e.g., HeyGen 3.0, Runway Gen-3) cannot run entirely locally without a dedicated multi-GPU cluster (e.g., 4× NVIDIA A100/H100 80GB).
   - Our local PyAV pipeline delivers **100% local, zero-cost compositing and live software screen recording**, while photorealistic AI human video generation relies on cloud rendering pipelines.
2. **Lip Synchronization Resolution:**
   - 2D image warping models (SadTalker) degrade at 1080p, producing dental blur. Premium commercial results require full-body 3D video generation (HeyGen/Runway) or professionally recorded spokesperson footage keyed over motion backdrops.
3. **Screen Recording Framerate vs. UI Tabular Data:**
   - Screen recordings of software interfaces with fine financial tables require high bitrates (8–10 Mbps) to avoid compression artifacts around small numbers and table borders.
4. **Data Ownership & Deployment Message Discipline (Section 6 Directive):**
   - The commercial strictly differentiates between **on-premises deployments** (where student records and financial ledgers reside solely on the institute's physical server) and **cloud/hybrid deployments** (where encrypted backups and WhatsApp gateways communicate externally). No misleading "zero third-party access" claims are made.
